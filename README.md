## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Pure Unit Converter** ("the App"), developed by **Mehdi Technologies**. This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to protecting user privacy and ensuring complete transparency.

Pure Unit Converter is an essential daily utility application designed to provide clean, accurate conversions across three core categories: **Weight**, **Length**, and **Temperature**.

By using the App, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

---

## 2. Zero Data Collection

**Pure Unit Converter does not collect, record, transmit, store, or share any personal information, device identifiers, or telemetry data.**

- ❌ **No Personal Data:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details.
- ❌ **No Location Data:** The app does not access, request, or track your GPS location or network location.
- ❌ **No Device Identifiers:** We do not track IMEI, MAC addresses, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), or hardware identifiers.
- ❌ **No Files or Media Access:** The app has no access to your photos, media, files, contacts, camera, or microphone.

All calculations and conversions are performed **100% locally on your device** using client-side mathematical algorithms.

---

## 3. Zero Special Permissions Required

Pure Unit Converter operates with **zero special Android permissions**. The application's `AndroidManifest.xml` does not declare or request:

- ❌ No `INTERNET` permission (fully disabled)
- ❌ No `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` or `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`
- ❌ No `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` or `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`
- ❌ No `CAMERA`, `RECORD_AUDIO`, or `READ_CONTACTS`

Because no internet permission is requested, the application is technically incapable of sending data off your device.

---

## 4. 100% Offline Functionality

The application functions completely offline without requiring any mobile data or Wi-Fi connection.

## 5. Advertisements & Third-Party Trackers

- **Zero Ads:** Pure Unit Converter contains absolutely no advertisements, sponsored banners, or ad networks (no Google AdMob, Unity Ads, etc.).
- **Zero Third-Party SDKs:** The app contains no third-party analytics, diagnostic, or tracking SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, or Sentry).

## 6. Children's Privacy

Pure Unit Converter is a general utility tool suitable for audiences of all ages, including children. Because the application collects no personal information whatsoever, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (**COPPA**) and the General Data Protection Regulation (**GDPR**).

## 7. Data Retention & Deletion

Since we collect no user data, there is no personal data retained on any external server. 

Any values entered into the input fields are stored only in temporary device memory during the active session and are cleared automatically when you navigate away, change units, or close the app.
